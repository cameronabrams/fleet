Fleet lifelines

One lane per role in the fleet. Each thin track is one transcript, from its first record to its last; the solid ticks on it are the 15-minute stretches in which that session wrote anything. A new track starts when a session is launched fresh or /cleared; a resumed session keeps writing to the same track.

Reading a lane

Several tracks in one lane are successive lives of the same role; tracks that overlap in time are stacked. Hover a track for its span, activity, messages and versions.

How identity was decided

From each transcript's own last name record. Where that name is retired, the transcript goes to the role the name became; where it recorded no name, to the role that owns its directory. Both are inferences and drawn dashed and faded.

What this does not show

Restarts that resume a transcript leave no break in a track. "Active" means some record was written in that 15 minutes, including while waiting on tools or jobs. Message counts come from transcripts and miss any whose transcript is gone.
